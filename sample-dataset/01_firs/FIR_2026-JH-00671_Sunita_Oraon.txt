5. Brief facts of the case (as narrated by complainant):
   Complainant states his daughter Sunita had been contacted approximately two
   weeks earlier by a woman from neighbouring Tupudana village known as "Kamla
   Aunty" who offered her a housekeeping job in Delhi at a monthly salary of
   Rs. 16,000 through a placement agency, "Shine Manpower Services", the same
   agency named in an earlier missing person case (FIR No. 2026/JH/00614 dated
   16/07/2026 registered at this very P.S. regarding one Priya Verma of
   Tupudana). Complainant became aware of this earlier case through village
   gossip only after his own daughter went missing, and states he did not
   initially suspect anything because Kamla Devi is well known and trusted in
   the area.

   On 02/08/2026, Sunita left home saying Kamla Aunty was taking her and one
   more girl to Ranchi railway station to catch a train to Delhi, and that a
   man would meet them there to help with tickets. She has not returned and her
   phone has been switched off since. Complainant states Kamla Devi's own
   house in Tupudana continues to remain locked and villagers have not seen her
   since mid-July 2026.

   Given the similarity to FIR No. 2026/JH/00614, complainant requests the two
   cases be investigated together as a possible organized racket.

6. Action taken:
   Case registered under Section 363 IPC; Section 370 IPC added on 06/08/2026
   after Investigating Officer noted the link to FIR No. 2026/JH/00614 and
   referred both cases jointly to the Anti-Human Trafficking Unit (AHTU),
   Ranchi for coordinated investigation.
