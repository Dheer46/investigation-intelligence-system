6. Brief facts of the case (as narrated by complainant):
   The complainant states that his daughter Priya Verma had been unemployed since
   completing her Class 12 examination and was actively looking for work. About
   three weeks prior to the incident, Priya received a phone call from a woman
   known in the locality as "Kamla Aunty" (later identified as Kamla Devi, wife of
   one Ramu Mahato, resident of Tupudana), who told her about a job opportunity
   for hotel/hospitality staff in Delhi through an agency called "Shine Manpower
   Services", offering a salary of Rs. 15,000-18,000 per month with free
   accommodation. Complainant states that Kamla Devi has arranged similar "city
   jobs" for two-three other girls in the village over the past year.

   On the morning of 14/07/2026, Priya informed her mother that Kamla Aunty had
   called her for "final documents and photo" before leaving for Delhi, and left
   home at approximately 08:00 hrs saying she would return by afternoon. She did
   not return. Her mobile phone has been switched off since approximately 11:00
   hrs the same day. Neighbours report seeing Priya boarding a shared auto-rickshaw
   near Tupudana Chowk along with an unknown male in the company of Kamla Devi at
   approximately 08:30 hrs.

   Complainant further states that Kamla Devi's phone is also not reachable since
   15/07/2026 and her house in Tupudana has been found locked since the same date.
   Complainant suspects his daughter has been misled and possibly trafficked under
   the pretext of a job offer, and requests urgent action.

7. Action taken:
   Case registered under Section 363 IPC. Investigation taken up by SI Anjali
   Kumari. Matter also flagged to the Anti-Human Trafficking Unit (AHTU), Ranchi
   for parallel intelligence input given the recruitment pattern described.
