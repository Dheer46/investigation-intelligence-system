INTELLIGENCE REPORT
Report No.: AHTU/RNC/2026/139                             Date: 09/08/2026
Subject: Follow-up on AHTU/RNC/2026/118 - CDR analysis and identification of
         Delhi-end contact; second missing-person case (Sunita Oraon) confirms
         pattern

1. Reference: AHTU/RNC/2026/118 dated 18/07/2026; FIR 2026/JH/00614 (Priya
   Verma); FIR 2026/JH/00671 (Sunita Oraon, registered 04/08/2026, same
   recruitment pattern via Kamla Devi, see attached FIR copy).

2. CDR analysis (numbers 9431187620 and 9871234567, period 01/06/2026 to
   05/08/2026), obtained from service provider under Section 91 Cr.P.C.
   request dated 20/07/2026:

   a) Number 9431187620 (subscriber: Kamla Devi, address Tupudana, Ranchi) was
      in frequent contact with victim Priya Verma's number (7903312456) in the
      days immediately preceding her disappearance on 14/07/2026, and similarly
      with victim Sunita Oraon's number (8210094721) in the days preceding her
      disappearance on 02/08/2026 - confirming Kamla Devi's direct role in both
      cases.

   b) The same number (9431187620) shows repeated contact, both before and on
      the dates of each disappearance, with number 9835512890. Local
      cell-tower data places 9835512890 in Namkum, Ranchi on both 14/07/2026
      and 02/08/2026 (dates of the two disappearances), and subsequently
      shows this number's location shifting toward Ranchi Railway Station on
      both dates within the same time window as the girls were last seen.
      Subscriber verification identifies this number as belonging to one
      Suresh Mahato, resident of Namkum, Ranchi - who field inquiry
      separately identifies as a cousin of Kamla Devi's husband Ramu Mahato.
      Suresh Mahato is assessed to be functioning as a local transporter,
      moving victims from the village to onward transport (rail/road) toward
      Delhi.

   c) Suresh Mahato's number (9835512890) is in turn in contact with number
      9871234567 on the same two dates (14/07/2026 and 02/08/2026) and the day
      following each (15/07/2026 and 03/08/2026), consistent with confirming
      "delivery" of each victim to the Delhi end. Subscriber/KYC verification
      for 9871234567 identifies the holder as Ashok Bhatia, proprietor of
      "Shine Manpower Services", registered office address Old Rohtak Road,
      Delhi. Cross-check with the Bihar Police Anti-Human Trafficking Unit,
      Munger, shows Ashok Bhatia (recorded there in 2024 records as "Ashok
      Kumar Bhatia") was questioned in a 2024 trafficking-recruitment case
      registered at Munger Town P.S. (case did not result in chargesheet for
      want of evidence at the time) involving an agency operating under a
      similar "manpower services" model. This is assessed as a significant
      corroborating link - Ashok Bhatia is a repeat person of interest in
      recruitment-linked trafficking cases.

3. Financial angle:
   A parallel request has been made to obtain bank statements for accounts
   held by Kamla Devi, Suresh Mahato, Ashok Bhatia, and the "Shine Manpower
   Services" business account to trace payment flows corroborating the
   recruiter/transporter/receiver roles identified above. See financial
   records forwarded separately.

4. Assessment:
   Evidence to date indicates an organized three-tier trafficking-recruitment
   structure:
     Recruiter (village-level): Kamla Devi
     Transporter (local movement): Suresh Mahato
     Receiver / placement front (Delhi): Ashok Bhatia, "Shine Manpower Services"
   Both known missing-person cases (Priya Verma, Sunita Oraon) fit this
   structure. Given Ashok Bhatia's prior flag in Munger, this unit assesses
   the network is not confined to Jharkhand and recommends notice to Delhi
   Police AHTU and Bihar AHTU for coordinated action.

5. Recommendations:
   a) Lookout circular for Kamla Devi and Suresh Mahato.
   b) Request Delhi Police to verify the registered address of "Shine
      Manpower Services" on Old Rohtak Road and ascertain if the two missing
      women are or were present there or at any linked premises.
   c) Freeze-request consideration for accounts linked to Ashok Bhatia and
      "Shine Manpower Services" pending financial analysis.
   d) Continue open-source monitoring of the "Shine Manpower Services"
      Facebook page for further victim recruitment activity.
