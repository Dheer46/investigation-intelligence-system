INTELLIGENCE REPORT
Report No.: AHTU/RNC/2026/118                             Date: 18/07/2026
Subject: Suspected recruitment pattern linked to "Shine Manpower Services" -
         two missing-women cases from Tupudana/Ormanjhi belt

1. Background:
   Following registration of FIR No. 2026/JH/00614 (P.S. Ranchi Sadar, dated
   16/07/2026) regarding missing person Priya Verma (22), this unit conducted a
   preliminary field inquiry in Tupudana and adjoining villages on 17-18/07/2026.

2. Findings:
   a) Local inquiry confirms that a woman identified as Kamla Devi (w/o Ramu
      Mahato), resident of Tupudana, has for the past 10-12 months been
      approaching young women in the area with job offers in Delhi/Mumbai on
      behalf of an agency named "Shine Manpower Services". She is locally
      referred to as "Kamla Aunty".

   b) Informant statement (identity withheld) states at least two other girls
      from Tupudana left the village in similar circumstances in the last year
      (approx. October 2025 and February 2026) after contact with Kamla Devi,
      and have not visited home since; families were reportedly told the girls
      were "doing well" in Delhi hotel jobs but calls to them go unanswered.

   c) Kamla Devi's residence in Tupudana has been found locked since
      approximately 15/07/2026, and her mobile number (9431187620) has been
      largely unreachable since that date, consistent with her having
      travelled out of Ranchi shortly after Priya Verma's disappearance.

   d) A Facebook page in the name "Shine Manpower Services - Delhi/Mumbai Jobs"
      was located during open-source checks, advertising hospitality and
      housekeeping jobs with the same salary range (Rs. 15,000-18,000/month)
      cited by the complainant in FIR 2026/JH/00614. The page lists a contact
      number 9871234567 with a Delhi STD-format display and additionally lists
      "Kamla Aunty (Ranchi contact) 9431187620" for candidates from the Ranchi
      region. Full capture attached separately as OSINT record.

3. Assessment:
   The recruitment pattern (local trusted contact approaching economically
   vulnerable young women with unverifiable placement agency jobs, followed by
   loss of contact) is consistent with known human trafficking recruitment
   methodology. This unit assesses MEDIUM-HIGH likelihood that Kamla Devi is
   functioning as a village-level recruiter for a larger network extending
   beyond Ranchi district, with the Delhi number 9871234567 warranting
   subscriber verification and CDR analysis.

4. Recommendations:
   a) Request CDR (call detail records) for 9431187620 (Kamla Devi) and
      9871234567 (unidentified Delhi contact) for the period 01/06/2026 to date.
   b) Request subscriber/KYC details for both numbers from respective service
      providers.
   c) Flag Kamla Devi and associated numbers for lookout at Ranchi Railway
      Station and Birsa Munda Airport.
   d) Cross-check "Shine Manpower Services" against MCA/state labour
      department placement agency registration records - preliminary check
      shows no valid registration found under this exact name in Jharkhand.

Reported by: Inspector Vikas Toppo, AHTU Ranchi
